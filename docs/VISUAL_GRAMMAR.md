# Visual Grammar Prototype — v0.3.4

This prototype reduces non-English cognitive load by making each interaction family visually distinct without adding new explanatory badges.

## Four visual grammars

### Direct
- Immediate one-choice response.
- Neutral surface.
- Large answer cards stacked vertically.
- No separate decision zone.

### Investigate
- Information Hunt, Troubleshooting, and Incident Investigation.
- Cool-toned workbench for questions, witnesses, causes, and evidence.
- A physically separated Decision Tray below the workbench.
- Question controls are rounded/pill-like; final decisions are squarer cards.

### Queue
- Rapid / multi-guest one-question-one-answer activities.
- Warm-toned workspace.
- A visual queue rail shows progress through guests.
- Current guest occupies a centered card; reply cards use a distinct asymmetric silhouette.

### Handoff
- Staff Coordination.
- Key facts are shown on a clipboard/paper surface.
- Selected notes remain on the clipboard.
- Final handoff sentence is chosen in a dark transfer tray below it.

## Design rule
Color is secondary. The primary signals are spatial structure, grouping, button silhouette, and persistent placement. A player should be able to infer the interaction family from the screen shape before reading instructions.
