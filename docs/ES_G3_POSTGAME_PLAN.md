# ES-G3 Postgame Plan — Exam Shift: Advanced

## Position in the game

ES-G3 is **not Chapter 9**.

After the player completes all 48 Level 1 Shifts, a separate postgame mode can unlock:

> **Exam Shift: Advanced**

The completion condition of this mode is **ES-G3 = 100%**.

This preserves the story/career arc of Level 1 while giving university-entrance and TOEIC learners a clear advanced goal.

## Why it fits the target audience

### University entrance
The postgame can emphasize:

- compressed and formal written structures
- participial constructions
- apposition
- nonrestrictive / preposition + relative structures
- inversion and cleft emphasis
- advanced conditionals
- time/aspect distinctions

### TOEIC L&R
TOEIC officially includes sentence completion, text completion, and single/multiple-passage reading in the Reading section, while Listening requires question-response, conversations and talks. English Shift should therefore train grammar **inside business/service context**, not as isolated translation exercises.

The postgame can emphasize:

- subject-verb agreement
- word form / parts of speech
- modifiers
- preposition vs conjunction choice
- pronoun reference
- sentence insertion / discourse flow
- fast interpretation of business notices, e-mails and staff conversations

## Proposed six modules

1. **Compressed English** — participles, apposition, advanced relatives
2. **Time & Aspect** — perfect infinitive, future progressive/perfect, passive infinitive
3. **Formal Structures** — cleft emphasis, inversion, formal conditionals
4. **Verb Patterns** — used to, causative have/get, perception patterns
5. **Comparison & Logic** — correlative comparison and advanced quantity expressions
6. **Exam Grammar Sprint** — word form, agreement, modifier, connector and reference decisions

## Gameplay

Reuse the four Visual Grammars instead of creating a new UI language:

- Direct: fast Part-5-like response/wording decisions
- Investigate: combine sentence clues before choosing the structure/meaning
- Queue: rapid mixed grammar decisions under time pressure
- Handoff: summarize business text while preserving grammatical relationships

The goal remains **English to accomplish a task**, even when the grammar is exam-oriented.
