# REPAIR LAB Curriculum

## Purpose

REPAIR LAB trains **grammar diagnosis**: noticing exactly what is wrong in a sentence and replacing only the broken part with a correct, natural form.

It is intentionally separate from Level 2 BUILD:
- BUILD asks the learner to construct English.
- REPAIR asks the learner to diagnose English.

## Curriculum

### Unit 1 — Sentence Foundations
do questions / demonstratives / there is-are / countability

### Unit 2 — Time & Tense
present progressive / past progressive / present perfect / present perfect progressive

### Unit 3 — Modals & Voice
should / must / may / passive voice

### Unit 4 — Verb Patterns
Would you mind + -ing / to-infinitive / object + to-infinitive / let + object + base verb

### Unit 5 — Questions & Clauses
indirect questions / relative pronouns / relative adverbs / whether clauses

### Unit 6 — Applied Grammar
past perfect / comparison / If I were you / SVOO

## UX rules

- Keep the broken area small: usually one chunk.
- Distractors should correspond to plausible learner errors.
- UI instructions and hints are Japanese.
- Target English remains natural customer-service English.
- After correction, always show the complete sentence and a short `Why?`.
- Do not expand FLOW LAB as part of REPAIR work.
