# Chapter 8 — International Flagship

## Purpose

Chapter 8 is the Level 1 final chapter. It introduces very little new grammar and instead mixes the language, decision skills, and Visual Grammar patterns learned in Chapters 1–7.

## Days 43–48

- Day 43: Complex Explanations — however / therefore / otherwise
- Day 44: Staff Briefing — reported information, noun clauses, concise handoff
- Day 45: Investigation — evidence, chronology, modal-perfect inference
- Day 46: VIP & Formal Service — polite register, indirect questions, policy boundaries
- Day 47: All-Store Gauntlet — cross-store information hunt, troubleshooting, queue
- Day 48: Final Shift — incident investigation, manager handoff, five-customer final queue

## Visual Grammar

All four v0.3.4 visual patterns are deliberately reused:

- Direct: one-response service decisions
- Investigate: gather information/evidence, then decide
- Queue: handle multiple customers sequentially
- Handoff: select essential facts, then relay them

## Level 1 Summary

After Day 48, Chapter 8 shows a Level 1 summary using saved local progress from all eight chapters. Because chapters are freely selectable, it distinguishes between:

- Final Chapter complete
- All 48 Level 1 shifts complete

It does not invent skill scores that were not historically stored. It reports only saved completion, best-day scores, and Japanese-hint usage.
